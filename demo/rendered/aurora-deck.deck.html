Why the kiosk exists

Note: staff cannot be everywhere

Visitors arrive curious and leave unbriefed whenever the floor is busy. The kiosk is the answer that never queues.

Showroom staff do their best work with visitors who already know what they want. The kiosk gets everyone to that point — on their own schedule.

The visitor journey

Three moves, shown here as cards. This slide carries its own data: the reveal var above drives the pacing in the slideshow renderer.

1. Discover

A short overview makes the choices visible before committing to a path.

2. Explore

Details appear when they are useful; the overview stays one action away.

3. Continue

The visitor leaves with a useful next step, not an obligation.

What it looks like

A good journey has an invitation, room to explore, and a clear way out.
